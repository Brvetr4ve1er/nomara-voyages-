NOMARA
Design System

«الرفيق» — Al-Rafiq, The Companion

One trusted companion, two journeys. This system encodes Nomara's Caregiver × Explorer identity as a single token architecture with two semantic modes: Rihla (رحلة — leisure, coral, energy) and Sakina (سكينة — Umrah & Hajj, gold, reverence). Same spine, different soul. Arabic-first, RTL-native, WhatsApp-centric.

01 · Foundations

Brand logic → system logic

The audit found one brand carrying two emotional registers: a family booking a once-in-a-lifetime Umrah, and a couple booking a week in Djerba. Splitting them into two brands would kill the cross-sell lifecycle. Forcing them into one visual register would make Umrah feel like a beach promo — or holidays feel like a sermon.

The answer is a dual-mode semantic layer. Primitives never change (teal spine, sand surfaces, type ramp). Semantic tokens remap per mode. A trip page for Sousse and a trip page for Shawwal Umrah are built from the identical components — only data-mode changes.

PrincipleMeaning in practice
Companion, not vendorThe founder-guide is the brand's core trust asset. UI always surfaces a human: guide photo near CTAs, real footage over stock, first-person voice ("نرافقك" — we accompany you).
Price is a featureTransparent pricing is their #1 rational hook. Prices are typographic heroes — big Changa numerals, DZD always visible, never hidden behind "contact us".
Arabic-first, French-fluentRTL is the default writing mode, not a patch. French/Latin destination names live inside Arabic layouts, like their existing creatives.
WhatsApp is checkoutNo fake e-commerce. The conversion primitive is a pre-filled WhatsApp deep link + call button. Components are designed around that reality.
One spine, two soulsTeal is constant. Coral = Rihla. Gold = Sakina. Changa is constant. Amiri enters only in Sakina. Modes share 80%, diverge 20% — exactly where emotion lives.

03 · Typography

Three voices, one conversation

Changa for display — a condensed Arabic+Latin face that matches the bold energy of their existing offer cards and makes DZD prices feel like headlines. Readex Pro for body — engineered specifically for Arabic on-screen readability, with a harmonized Latin. Amiri for reverence — a Naskh serif that enters only in Sakina mode, for headings and du'a quotes.

05 · Signature device

The Arch — Sakina's silent marker

One graphic device tells the user which world they're in before they read a word. In Sakina mode, hero and card images are cropped with an Islamic arch mask (--radius-arch) — evoking the Haram's gates without pasting lantern clipart everywhere. In Rihla mode, the same images are simple rounded rectangles. Subtle, ownable, and impossible to mistake for a template.

Rihla — rounded rect, full saturation

Sakina — arch mask, calmer grade

Arch as outlined frame — quotes, du'a, guide intro

Rules: the arch is exclusive to Sakina. Never on promo badges, never on leisure content, never more than one arch-framed element per viewport. Sakina imagery gets a −15% saturation, +slight warm grade; Rihla imagery stays vivid.

08 · RTL & bilingual

Arabic is the grid, French is a guest

RuleSpec
Document<html lang="ar" dir="rtl"> default. French toggle flips to lang="fr" dir="ltr" — full mirror, not patched margins.
CSSLogical properties ONLY: margin-inline-start, inset-inline-end, text-align:start. Zero left/right in the codebase.
Embedded LatinDestination names & prices inside Arabic text: wrap in <bdi> or dir="ltr" spans. Phone numbers always dir="ltr".
NumeralsWestern Arabic numerals (0–9) everywhere — matches their existing creatives and price-comparison behavior.
IconsDirectional icons (arrows, chevrons) mirror in RTL. Plane/star/calendar do not.
FontsChanga & Readex Pro & Amiri all carry harmonized Latin — never introduce a separate French font.

Shipping notes

TopicDecision
